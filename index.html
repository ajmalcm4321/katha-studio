About the model and “full freedom”

Model: AI4Bharat Indic Parler-TTS, model weights published under Apache 2.0. The model supports Malayalam and lists Anjali, Anju and Harish as Malayalam speakers. The Hugging Face repository may ask you to sign in and accept its access conditions before downloading the weights. Apache 2.0 does not remove copyright restrictions on the books you upload.

No API quota: once the weights are downloaded, inference runs on your own GPU/CPU server. You still need compute, electricity, internet to connect to a remote server, and storage. A free hosted notebook can disconnect or impose session limits.
